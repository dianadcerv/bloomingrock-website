# LinkedIn Company Page — setup pack

Create the page here (signed into your personal LinkedIn):  
https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/

Use this pack so you can paste fields in under 10 minutes.

---

## 1. Create the page

1. Open the link above (or LinkedIn → **For Business** → **Create a Company Page**).
2. Choose **Company**.
3. Page identity / name: **BloomingRock Solutions**
4. LinkedIn public URL (vanity): `bloomingrock-solutions`  
   (If taken, try `bloomingrocksolutions` or `bloomingrock-llc`.)
5. Website: `https://www.bloomingrocksolutions.com`
6. Check that you’re an authorized representative → **Create page**.

---

## 2. Paste these fields


### Industry

Best fit: **Business Consulting and Services**

### Company size

**Myself only** (or 1 employee / 2–10 if LinkedIn forces a range)

### Company type

**Privately Held**

### Location

Your primary city/region (add when prompted)

## 4. After the page exists

1. **Add your personal profile as admin** (you’ll already be).
2. On your personal profile → **Experience** → add **BloomingRock Solutions** and link the Company Page.
3. Post once as the company (or as you, tagging the page):

```
Most small business owners don’t need more AI tools.

They need one workflow set up around how they already work — so it actually gets used.

That’s what BloomingRock does:
Audit → Quick Win → Handoff

If your week is full of repetitive follow-up, scheduling, or admin, I’m offering a free 15-minute look.

→ www.bloomingrocksolutions.com
```

4. Invite connections who own/run small businesses to follow the page (start with 10–20, not everyone).

**Clear-space rule:** Keep empty margin around the mark at least equal to the height of the bloom blossom.

**Do not:** stretch, add drop shadows, recolor arbitrarily, or place the dark wordmark on dark photos without a light scrim.

**On dark backgrounds:** use the transparent mark/wordmark with light text (`#F6F5F1`) or invert stone paths to `#C4C0B6` and bloom to `#9BB59D`.

## Voice

- Short sentences. Practical. No jargon walls.
- Primary CTA: **Book a free 15-minute look**
- Package story: **Audit → Quick Win → Handoff**
- Elevator pitch (3 sentences):

> I help small business owners find one or two places in their business where AI can save them real time — like customer follow-up, scheduling, or admin work — then I set it up for them so it actually gets used. Most owners know they should be using AI but don't have time to figure out which tool or how to install it. I do that part for them.

## Visual language

- Metaphor: growth through stone — steady growth + resilience  
- Hero imagery: wild growth / lichen / bloom on rock ledge  
- Motion (site): slow ken-burns, soft fog drift, fade-up — never flashy
